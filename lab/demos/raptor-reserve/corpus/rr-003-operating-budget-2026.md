# Operating Budget 2026

Category: Governance
Date: 2026-01-12
Author: Dr. Mara Voss
Source: Raptor Reserve internal record

The 2026 operating budget is USD 1,840,000, a 6.4 percent increase over 2025. The increase covers two seasonal research assistants, a new acoustic sensor array, and repairs to the South Beach boardwalk after storm Oscar.

Spending is split across five programs: conservation 41 percent, research 22 percent, education 14 percent, facilities 17 percent, and administration 6 percent. Staff costs are 54 percent of the total. The contingency line is 4 percent of the operating budget.

Revenue comes from grants (38 percent), individual donations (27 percent), the endowment (19 percent), program fees (9 percent), and other sources (7 percent). The largest single grant is USD 240,000 from the Maine Coast Trust for coastal habitat work.

The board approved a projected surplus of USD 12,000. The finance committee will review the budget in July and October. Any line that moves by more than 10 percent needs board approval.

The capital budget is separate. It covers the lighthouse roof, the solar battery replacement, and the new boat engine. The capital budget for 2026 is USD 310,000.

## Scope

Operating Budget 2026 applies to the board, the director, full-time and seasonal staff, volunteers, researchers, and contractors working on Blacktail Island. It does not replace the charter (rr-001), the emergency response plan (rr-006), or the safety plan (rr-005). When two documents disagree, the charter wins, then the management plan, then the specific policy.

## Roles and responsibilities

The board approves the document. The director, Dr. Mara Voss, owns implementation and reports exceptions to the board. The head ranger, Elin Ward, is the day-to-day contact for field staff. The data manager, Samir Haddad, keeps the signed copy in the document register and makes sure the shared drive has the current version.

## What staff should do

Read the document during onboarding. Ask the director or the head ranger if a situation is not covered. Record decisions that deviate from the document, including the reason and the date. Do not edit the shared copy. Send proposed changes to the data manager.

## Records and evidence

The reserve keeps the approved version, the board minutes, the public comment summary, and the annual review memo. Records are kept for 10 years. Personnel details are stored separately and are not part of the public corpus.

## Review and amendment

Review follows the schedule in the document. If the document does not set one, the default is every two years. Proposed changes go to the board packet 14 days before the meeting. A charter change needs a two-thirds vote; other documents need a simple majority.

## Common failure modes

The most common problems are outdated copies, unclear ownership, and field decisions that are never written down. The reserve fixes these by naming one owner, using a single document register, and reviewing the field log at the Monday safety meeting.
