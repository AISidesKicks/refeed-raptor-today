# Staff Handbook

Category: Governance
Date: 2026-02-01
Author: Elin Ward
Source: Raptor Reserve internal record

The staff handbook explains how work happens on Blacktail Island. Full-time staff work a 40-hour week. Seasonal staff work 12 to 20 weeks, usually from April to September. Volunteers are covered by the volunteer handbook and work with a named supervisor.

The day starts with a 7:30 stand-up at the research station. Each team reports weather, wildlife, safety, and the day's plan. The radio channel for operations is channel 2. Channel 3 is reserved for emergencies. Staff who work alone must check in at 10:00 and 14:00.

Housing is available in eight cabins near Seal Cove. The cabins have shared kitchens and a strict quiet policy after 22:00. Pets are not allowed on the island. Staff may bring a boat only with the maintenance lead's approval.

Uniforms are required for visitor-facing work. Staff should report damaged equipment, wildlife injuries, and unsafe conditions the same day. The reserve follows a no-blame reporting policy for near misses, but repeated safety violations can lead to dismissal.

The handbook is reviewed every two years. The current version took effect on 1 February 2026.

## Scope

Staff Handbook applies to the board, the director, full-time and seasonal staff, volunteers, researchers, and contractors working on Blacktail Island. It does not replace the charter (rr-001), the emergency response plan (rr-006), or the safety plan (rr-005). When two documents disagree, the charter wins, then the management plan, then the specific policy.

## Roles and responsibilities

The board approves the document. The director, Dr. Mara Voss, owns implementation and reports exceptions to the board. The head ranger, Elin Ward, is the day-to-day contact for field staff. The data manager, Samir Haddad, keeps the signed copy in the document register and makes sure the shared drive has the current version.

## What staff should do

Read the document during onboarding. Ask the director or the head ranger if a situation is not covered. Record decisions that deviate from the document, including the reason and the date. Do not edit the shared copy. Send proposed changes to the data manager.

## Records and evidence

The reserve keeps the approved version, the board minutes, the public comment summary, and the annual review memo. Records are kept for 10 years. Personnel details are stored separately and are not part of the public corpus.

## Review and amendment

Review follows the schedule in the document. If the document does not set one, the default is every two years. Proposed changes go to the board packet 14 days before the meeting. A charter change needs a two-thirds vote; other documents need a simple majority.

## Common failure modes

The most common problems are outdated copies, unclear ownership, and field decisions that are never written down. The reserve fixes these by naming one owner, using a single document register, and reviewing the field log at the Monday safety meeting.
